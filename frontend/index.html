encrypted records

Password Vault

Vault fields are encrypted in your browser with AES-GCM before they are saved. The server stores ciphertext only. Use a strong passphrase and keep a safe backup; forgetting it means the entries cannot be decrypted.

Enter a master passphrase to unlock or initialize the vault.
